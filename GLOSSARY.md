# Kingdom

Kingdom coordinates models, tools and connected computers to complete work with verifiable results.

## Language

**Kingdom**:
The whole coordinated system, including its connected computers, agents, shared knowledge and work.
_Avoid_: A synonym for one computer or one agent

**Knight**:
A computer participating in a Kingdom. A Knight can host several Apprentices.
_Avoid_: Coder agent, model, software worker

**Apprentice**:
An agent hosted on a Knight, with a role and granted tools for completing work.
_Avoid_: Knight-coder, computer, node when referring to an agent

**Coordination hierarchy**:
The reporting and delegation relationships between participants. A change in coordination does not change which computer physically hosts an Apprentice.
_Avoid_: Physical network, hosting hierarchy

**Autonomy level**:
The owner's authority policy for how work proceeds and when approval is required. It is independent of the participant's position in the coordination hierarchy.
_Avoid_: Hierarchy level, graph depth

**King Knight (King)**:
The single Knight designated by the human owner to govern a Kingdom. Being offline does not vacate the crown.
_Avoid_: Owner account, elected-on-disconnect leader

**Knight Commander**:
A Knight entrusted with authority over its assigned groups when there is no King, or under the King's explicit delegation.
_Avoid_: Any group organizer, unrestricted administrator

**Ghost Key**:
The optional, single mobile credential with the King's authority. It is distinct from ordinary mobile pairing and is off until explicitly established.
_Avoid_: Backup owner password, automatically issued mobile session
